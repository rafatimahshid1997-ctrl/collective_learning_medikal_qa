## Key Improvements Observed Across Iterations:

### Medical Correctness
- **Iteration 0**: Frequent hallucinations, incorrect dosages, wrong treatment recommendations
- **Iteration 1**: Basic correctness achieved, but lacks nuance
- **Iteration 2**: Medically accurate with important details included
- **Iteration 3**: Comprehensive, accurate, with appropriate caveats and statistical context

### Safety
- **Iteration 0**: Dangerous recommendations (self-medication, unnecessary panic)
- **Iteration 1**: Safe but sometimes overly cautious
- **Iteration 2**: Balanced safety with appropriate urgency levels
- **Iteration 3**: Clear safety warnings with specific criteria for when to seek emergency care

### Clarity and Structure
- **Iteration 0**: Brief, often confusing
- **Iteration 1**: Clear but basic
- **Iteration 2**: Well-structured with bullet points
- **Iteration 3**: Highly organized with sections, clear action items, and context

### Use of Uncertainty Language
- **Iteration 0**: Overconfident, gives definitive answers inappropriately
- **Iteration 1**: Some hedging introduced
- **Iteration 2**: Appropriate use of "consult a doctor"
- **Iteration 3**: Explicitly states limitations ("I cannot diagnose"), provides probabilities, distinguishes between likely and unlikely scenarios

### Handling of Misinformation
- **Iteration 0**: Contains misinformation and potentially harmful advice
- **Iteration 1**: Corrects major errors but may lack detail
- **Iteration 2**: Scientifically accurate with important warnings
- **Iteration 3**: Addresses common misconceptions explicitly, provides evidence-based information with statistics
